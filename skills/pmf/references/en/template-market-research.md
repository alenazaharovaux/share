## Filling guidance

- **Time-box research:** 2-4 hours per dimension. Do not go down rabbit holes.
- **Threshold for "enough":** when new searches start repeating the same things. That is the saturation signal.
- **Equal coverage:** all 7 dimensions, not just Customer + Problem (the most common mistake).
- **Antilogs are mandatory:** without antilogs research = wishful thinking. At least 1 antilog per dimension.
- **Concrete sources:** not "according to the internet", but a concrete URL + date.
