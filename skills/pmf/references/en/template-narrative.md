## Notes for filling

- **V1**: write without pressure, fill what you know, explicitly mark Weak evidence
- **V2**: after market research — update evidence levels, add competitor analogs/antilogs into the Validation table
- **V3**: after field interviews — rewrite Customer and Problem in user language with real quotes in the comments

**Size:**
- V1: 600-1000 words
- V2: 800-1200 words
- V3: 1000-1500 words

If less — not enough specificity. If more — details belong in research/interview docs, not the narrative.
