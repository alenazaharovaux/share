# Before → after

Pairs from real texts the skill author commented on, translated from Russian. Add new pairs after every round of comments.

## Article 07 of a course on AI agents for researchers, November 2026

**Chopped sentence**
- Before: "The step is needed for line numbers. Then every quote will refer to transcript lines…"
- After: "One format is needed so every quote has a reliable address. Later the agent will cite a quote by its line number, and a script will check the quote against that number. If the whole conversation is stored as one line, a line number tells you nothing."

**Reference to something unseen**
- Before: "The other question gives a different answer. The second question is a reconstruction-interview technique."
- After: "The interviewer asks: 'Go back to the day you got this card. What happened then?' … Such a question is a reconstruction-interview technique (switch interview): the interviewer takes the person back to the moment of the decision."

**Pretty phrase**
- Before: "When the model reads everyone at once, it looks for the common pattern and smooths out whatever sticks out."
- After: "r03 did not come back to 'Plate' because she collects loyalty points in a rival food-delivery app. If several other respondents did not come back because of slow delivery, a model reading everyone at once will most likely write 'customers do not return because of delivery' and lose r03's points."

**Rule without a consequence**
- Before: "One more rule: one action per link. If three actions are recorded as one link, the details of each are lost."
- After: "Line 26 has three difficulties: sign-up, the address, the minimum order. In synthesis each goes into its own theme. If the remark is recorded as one link, 'the first order was hard', it lands in one theme only, and two of the three difficulties never appear in the findings."

**Line number without a quote**
- Before: "High — … r03 talks about points in lines 14, 42, 48 and 50."
- After: the same, plus the text: "Line 48: 'Value. I count money. If everything is the same, I go where I get something back.'"

**Finding without a person**
- Before: "A competitor's loyalty programme lowers the likelihood of a repeat order."
- After: "r03 reorders in the rival app because she collects points there and spends them on taxis. 'Plate' has no points, and r03 sees no reason to come back (line 42)."

**Label without an action**
- Before: "'Needed from the observer' — what was on the screen, gestures and facial expression."
- After: "'Needed from the observer' means: the researcher asks the observer or checks the recording to see what was on the screen and how the person reacted, and adds it to the analysis."

## A website about older people and ATMs, September 2026

- Before: "The outcome of a banking operation for an older person is determined by the situation around it." Comment: "Around what? Around the operation? What kind of phrase is this?"
- Rule: a concrete subject; a pronoun is replaced with what it points to.

## A research synthesis, October 2026

- Before: "The difference lies in having a goal and lacking an alternative", and after a "humanizer" pass, "whether there was somewhere to retreat to". Comment: "What does 'somewhere to retreat to' mean? Is this a battlefield?"
- After: "Respondent A was saving for a flat. Respondent B paid a deposit on a new car."

## A curator's talk, November 2026

- Before: "The anonymised clone is made as a separate site." Comment: "what does that mean?"
- After: "This shows an anonymised copy: the agent removed the client's name and the private findings and published the copy at a separate address. The client's original stays behind a password, unchanged."
- Before: "GitHub trends radar: a daily snapshot of repositories, growth per day, weekly digests." Comment: "why weren't GitHub trends enough for me, why did I need my own radar"
- After: "The regular GitHub trends show what is popular today. My radar keeps watching every repository that has ever come into view, even after it has dropped out of the trends."
